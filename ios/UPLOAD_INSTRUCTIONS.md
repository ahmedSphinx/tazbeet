### Upload to App Store Connect

#### Method 1: App Store Connect Website
1. Go to [appstoreconnect.apple.com](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com)
2. Sign in with your Apple Developer account
3. Click "My Apps" → select Tazbeet
4. Click "Prepare for Submission"
5. Drag and drop `tazbeet.ipa` to upload area
6. Complete metadata and screenshots
7. Submit for review

#### Method 2: Xcode Organizer
1. Open Xcode
2. Window → Organizer → Archives
3. Select your archive
4. Click "Distribute App"
5. Follow on-screen instructions

#### Method 3: Transporter
1. Download Transporter from Mac App Store
2. Open Transporter app
3. Drag and drop `tazbeet.ipa`
4. Follow upload process
